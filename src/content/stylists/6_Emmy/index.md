Emmys journey began as soon as she was old enough to pick up an eye shadow palette. She knew early she had a passion for beauty and helping others achieve their most beautiful true self. She graduated from Douglas J Aveda Institute in Ann Arbor Mi and began her career in 2022. From Blondes and Brunettes to hair extensions and make up artistry, she strives to make it a luxurious experience for everyone. Outside of work Emmy loves to travel, spend time with family and friends and snuggle with her cat Arabella.
